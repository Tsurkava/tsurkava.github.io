Can a foreigner buy an apartment in Batumi?

Yes. Foreigners can buy apartments, houses, commercial property and non-agricultural land in Georgia on the same terms as Georgian citizens. The only restriction is agricultural land. No residence permit is needed to buy.

Can I buy property in Batumi remotely?

Yes. Viewings can be done by video, and the purchase can be signed by a representative under a notarized power of attorney. Tsurkava Property supports remote buyers through the whole process.

Does buying property in Georgia give a residence permit?

Yes, if the property is worth at least USD 150,000 (threshold in force since 1 March 2026; it was USD 100,000 before). This gives a one-year renewable residence permit. A property investment of USD 300,000 or more can qualify for a 5-year investment residence permit.

Is there a property transfer tax in Georgia?

No. Georgia has no transfer tax or stamp duty on buying real estate. The buyer pays only registration fees at the Public Registry and, if used, notary fees.

How long does property registration take in Georgia?

Usually from one to four business days. Ownership is registered by the National Agency of Public Registry, typically through a Public Service Hall; faster registration is available for a higher fee.
